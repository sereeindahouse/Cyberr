**How you use this in real life:**
> 
>         **You run ==crt.sh== and discover dev-internal-api.targetcompany.com that a developer created 3 weeks ago and forgot to delete.**
> 
>         **You check ==DNSDumpster== and discover an old server hosted on an unmonitored IP block.**
> 
>         **You check ==Shodan== and find that their secondary office in Germany has an exposed database on ==Port 3306== with no firewall rule.**
> 
>         **You check their DNS ==TXT== records, see that their ==DMARC== policy is set to p=none, and you execute a phishing simulation against their employees.**
